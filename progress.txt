Where things stand (2026-11-15, evening)

E7 — pocket-centric matched ablation — is DONE, and it is NULL.

What was blocking it: the _fpocket wrapper wrote an EMPTY PDB. Bio.PDB.PDBIO.set_structure()
wraps a *copy* of a Model, so the id()-based residue filter rejected every residue; fpocket
printed "contains no atoms" and the wrapper silently returned 0 pockets (every fp_* column = 0).
Fixed in features/pocket_ensemble.py: the PDB is now hand-written with residues numbered by
UniProt index; an fpocket failure now raises and is counted per conformer (NaN, not 0); per-
conformer rows are saved with a holo flag (pocket_conformers.csv); an apo-vs-holo cryptic flag
(x_cryptic_apo_holo) was added alongside the AF-vs-experiment one. The broken-run shards were
moved to $S2D_FEATURES/pocket_ensemble/shards_broken_fpocket_20261115/.

Jobs (lov4, chained afterok): 111540 profile (40 shards, ~10 min) -> 111541 gather -> 111542 E7.

AF pockets overlap the ligand site. Cryptic: AF-vs-exp 48% (inflated by induced fit — holo chains
are ligand-stripped), apo-vs-holo 30% of the 93 targets that have both.
